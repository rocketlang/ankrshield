## 💰 Pricing Strategy

### Free (OSS) - The Hook
**Target:** Individual users, tech enthusiasts, open source advocates

**Value Prop:**
- "Privacy protection for everyone"
- Fully functional desktop app
- No credit card required
- Community-driven

**Limitations (by design):**
- Desktop only (no mobile)
- 1 device only
- Basic 50k blocklist
- 7-day history
- No cloud sync
- Community support only

**Goal:**
- 100,000+ users in Year 1
- Build trust and awareness
- Create community advocates
- Gather feedback

---

### Freemium - The Taste
**Target:** Mobile users, multi-device households

**Value Prop:**
- "Take your privacy everywhere"
- Mobile apps included
- Cloud sync across devices
- Larger blocklist (100k)

**Catch:**
- Upgrade nags every 7 days
- "Pro features locked" indicators
- Limited to 5 devices
- 30-day history only

**Conversion Strategy:**
```
Day 7:  "Enjoying ankrshield? Upgrade to Pro for VPN!"
Day 14: "Your privacy score improved 23%! Go Pro for deeper insights"
Day 21: "Pro users get custom blocklists. Want to try?"
Day 30: "Limited time: 2 months free with annual Pro plan!"
```

**Goal:**
- 10,000+ freemium users in Year 1
- 20% conversion to Pro within 90 days

---

### Pro - The Sweet Spot
**Target:** Privacy-conscious families, power users, small businesses

**Pricing:**
- $9.99/month
- $99/year (17% savings - 2 months free)

**Value Prop:**
- "Complete privacy for your family"
- VPN included (saves $5-10/month vs competitors)
- 10 devices (cover whole family)
- Advanced customization
- Priority support

**Revenue Model:**
```
10,000 Pro users @ $99/year = $990,000/year
Monthly: $82,500 MRR
Annual run rate: $1M ARR
```

**Goal:**
- 10,000 Pro users in Year 1
- $1M ARR by end of Year 1
- 30% gross margin (after infra costs)

---

### Enterprise - High Margin
**Target:** Companies 100-10,000 employees, schools, universities

**Pricing:**
- $499/month (100 seats) = $5/user/month
- $999/month (500 seats) = $2/user/month
- $4,999/month (5,000 seats) = $1/user/month
- Custom for 10,000+ seats

**Value Prop:**
- "Privacy compliance made easy"
- GDPR/CCPA compliance assistance
- On-premise deployment option
- SSO integration
- Audit logs for compliance
- Dedicated support + SLA

**Target Customers:**
- Privacy-focused companies (lawyers, healthcare)
- Educational institutions
- Government agencies
- Financial services
- Remote-first companies

**Goal:**
- 50 enterprise customers in Year 1
- $2-5M ARR from enterprise
- 70% gross margin (high touch sales)

### Free → Freemium (33% conversion)
```
Trigger: User wants mobile app

1. Download app from store
2. Sign in with desktop account
3. Cloud sync enabled automatically

### Freemium → Pro (20% conversion)
```

1. Hit 5-device limit or see VPN feature
2. "Upgrade to Pro" prompt
